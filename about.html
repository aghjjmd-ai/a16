About Dinette

For the decisions around the recipe.

Dinette is a small, independent site about the everyday questions of dinner: what goes with what, how much to make, and what to do with the rest.

Why these questions

Recipes are easy to find. The harder part is everything a recipe leaves out. It will tell you how to roast a chicken, but not what to serve beside it, whether one bird feeds six, or how to turn the remains into Tuesday’s dinner.

How we write

From home cooking, checked against published food-safety guidance on cooling, storing and reheating, and read by a second person before it is published. Quantities are sensible averages; appetites differ, and we say so.

What we avoid

We sell nothing, accept no payment for mentions and give no medical or dietary advice. If you find a mistake, tell us through the contact page.
